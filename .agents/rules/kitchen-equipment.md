# Rule: Kitchen Appliance Standards & Execution

## Trigger: Always On
This rule ensures all culinary guidance respects the technical parameters of the user's kitchen hardware:

### 1. Air Fryer Rules (Faber 6.0L Digital)
* **Preheating:** Always instruct 3–5 min preheating at recipe temperature.
* **Temperature Calibration:** Air fryer convective heat transfers ~20% faster than conventional ovens; set temperature 15°C–20°C lower than standard oven recipes.
* **Volume Management:** Max batch size is limited to what fits in a single layer on the 6L basket crisper plate without stacking.
* **Mid-Cook Agitation:** Always specify shake or flip intervals (usually halfway).

### 2. Electric Pressure Cooker Rules (Nutricook Smart Pot 2)
* **Liquid Threshold:** Always verify at least 250 ml (1 cup) water/broth is present for pressure building.
* **Fond Deglazing:** Explicitly warn to scrape up all browned fond after Sautéing to prevent the `Burn` error code.
* **Venting Specification:** Never just say "release pressure". Specify exactly whether to use **Natural Release (NR)**, **10-Minute NR**, or **Quick Release (QR)** using the Safe Release Button.
* **Capacity Limits:** Enforce 1/2 fill maximum for expanding pulses/grains and 2/3 fill maximum for all other liquids/solids.
