# Nutricook Smart Pot 2 Operating Skill

## 1. Hardware Overview & Specifications

* **Model:** Nutricook Smart Pot 2 (Model NC-SP204K series)
* **Capacity:** 6 Litres / 6 Quarts (Standard family volume; also applies to 8L/9.5L Plus models)
* **Power Rating:** 1000 Watts (220V–240V AC, 50/60 Hz)
* **Inner Pot Material:** Heavy-duty Food Grade 304 (18/8) Stainless Steel with encapsulated tri-ply aluminum base; zero chemical coatings, durable, dishwasher-safe
* **Lid Engineering:**
  * **Auto-Sealing Smart Lid:** Automatically sets to "Sealing" position upon locking
  * **Safe Pressure Release Button:** Dedicated push-button remote steam discharge positioned safely away from the steam exhaust nozzle to protect hands from scalding
  * **Float Valve:** Visual indicator of internal pressure (up = pressurized/locked; down = depressurized/safe to open)
* **Operational Modes:** 9-in-1 / 12-in-1 multi-cooker (Pressure Cook, Sauté, Slow Cook, Rice, Steam, Soup/Broth, Yogurt, Sous Vide, Keep Warm, Meat/Stew, Bean/Lentil, Biryani, Porridge)
* **Pressure Settings:** High Pressure (10.2–11.6 psi / 70–80 kPa, ~115°C–118°C) and Low Pressure (5.8–7.2 psi / 40–50 kPa, ~110°C)
* **Smart Adjust Levels:** `Less`, `Normal`, `More` adjust temperature/time presets for customized textures

## 2. Fundamental Safety & Liquid Rules

### A. The Minimum Liquid Rule
* **Mandatory Requirement:** The Smart Pot 2 requires at least **1 cup (250 ml)** of thin, watery liquid (water, broth, stock, thin tomato water) to generate steam and build pressure.
* **Thick Liquids Alert:** Cream, dairy, thick tomato puree, coconut cream, or cornstarch slurry should **never** be placed at the bottom before pressurization—they will scorch and trigger the `Burn` warning. Stir dairy and thickeners in after pressure cooking using `Sauté` mode.

### B. Fill Level Maximums
1. **Regular Foods:** Never fill above the **2/3 MAX** line stamped on the stainless pot.
2. **Expanding Foods (Rice, Beans, Dal, Grains, Dehydrated Veg):** Never fill above the **1/2 line** to leave headroom for foam expansion.
3. **Applesauce / Cranberries / Pearl Barley:** Avoid pressure cooking foods prone to extreme foaming that can clog the steam release pipe and anti-block shield.

## 3. Pressure Release Protocols

Selecting the correct pressure release method prevents food toughening, boiling over, or mushy textures:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        PRESSURE RELEASE METHODS                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. NATURAL RELEASE (NR)                                                │
│    • Procedure: Do nothing when timer reaches 0. Allow pot to cool     │
│      naturally until float valve drops (15–30 minutes).                │
│    • Best For: Large meat cuts, bone broths, rajma, chana, high-foaming│
│      soups. Prevents violent boiling that toughens meat or spews foam. │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. 10-MINUTE NATURAL RELEASE (10m NR -> QR)                            │
│    • Procedure: Let pot rest under natural release for exactly 10 min, │
│      then press the Safe Pressure Release Button to exhaust residue.   │
│    • Best For: Chicken breasts, biryani, pulao, white rice, pasta.     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. QUICK RELEASE (QR)                                                  │
│    • Procedure: Immediately push the Safe Pressure Release Button to   │
│      vent steam continuously until float valve drops (2–3 minutes).    │
│    • Best For: Delicate vegetables (broccoli, zucchini), seafood, corn,│
│      eggs, quick-steamed items to stop cooking immediately.            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 4. Key Functions & Master Workflows

### A. Sauté -> Deglaze -> Pressure Cook (The Golden Rule)
1. Select **Sauté** (toggle between `Less`, `Normal`, `More` for low simmer vs sear).
2. Heat oil, brown meats, fry whole spices, or caramelize onions directly in the stainless pot with the **lid off**.
3. **CRITICAL STEP - Deglaze:** Add a splash of liquid and use a wooden spoon to firmly scrape off all browned bits (fond) stuck to the bottom of the pot. **Failure to deglaze is the #1 cause of the "Burn" error**.
4. Add remaining ingredients and required liquid, lock the Smart Lid, select **Pressure Cook**, and dial the timer.

### B. Sous Vide Precision Cooking
1. Place food in food-grade silicone or BPA-free vacuum bags with seasonings.
2. Fill stainless pot with warm water between the MIN and 1/2 lines.
3. Select **Sous Vide**, set desired temperature (e.g., 54°C for medium-rare steak, 63°C for tender chicken), and time.
4. Lower bagged food into the preheated water using water displacement. Place lid loosely on top.

### C. Yogurt Making
1. **Pasteurize:** Pour milk into pot, select **Yogurt -> Boil**. Heat to 82°C (180°F).
2. **Cool:** Let milk cool down to 43°C (110°F).
3. **Inoculate & Incubate:** Whisk in 2 tbsp fresh yogurt starter (active cultures). Select **Yogurt -> Normal**, set timer for 8–10 hours. Do not disturb the pot.
4. **Chill:** Transfer to refrigerator for 4–6 hours to set firmness.

## 5. Cleaning & Maintenance Runbook

1. **Inner Pot:** 304 Stainless steel is dishwasher-safe and scrub-resistant. For stubborn water stains or rainbow discoloration, boil 1 cup water with 1 cup white vinegar on `Sauté` for 5 minutes.
2. **Silicone Gasket Ring:** Remove ring from lid after every use. Wash with warm soapy water. To remove curry odors, soak in warm water with lemon juice or steam 2 cups water + 1 tbsp vinegar for 5 min.
3. **Anti-Block Shield & Float Valve:** Pop off the oval anti-block shield on the lid underside; rinse clear of food debris. Check that float valve moves freely.
4. **Condensation Collector:** Slide out the small plastic cup at the back of the cooker, empty moisture, and snap back.
