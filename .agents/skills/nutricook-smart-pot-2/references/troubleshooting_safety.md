# Nutricook Smart Pot 2: Troubleshooting & Safety Guide

This guide covers common error codes, physical safety protocols, and operational solutions for the Nutricook Smart Pot 2.

---

## 1. Error Codes & Fixes

### A. The "Burn" or "Overheat" Warning
* **Symptoms:** Cooker beeps continuously and displays `Burn` or stops heating.
* **Root Causes:**
  1. Food is scorched onto the bottom of the stainless steel inner pot.
  2. Not enough thin liquid in the pot (minimum 250 ml required).
  3. Starch/sugary sauce (tomato puree, barbecue sauce, cornstarch) was added before pressurization without floating on top.
* **Resolution:**
  1. Press **Cancel** and wait for pressure to release or push the Safe Release Button.
  2. Open lid, remove food, and inspect the pot bottom.
  3. Soak and thoroughly scrape away burned residue.
  4. Always **deglaze** the bottom after sautéing. Pour water/broth first, and layer thick tomato purees **on top** without stirring before pressure cooking.

### B. Steam Leaking Around Lid Rim
* **Root Causes:**
  1. Silicone sealing ring not properly seated in the lid wire track.
  2. Gasket ring is damaged, cracked, or dirty.
  3. Food debris on the pot rim or gasket.
* **Resolution:** Cool down, wash gasket, dry completely, and press firmly into the lid channel. Replace gasket if stretched out.

### C. Float Valve Does Not Rise
* **Root Causes:**
  1. Not enough liquid inside to generate sufficient steam pressure.
  2. Safe pressure release button is engaged or stuck open.
  3. Lid is not fully rotated and locked into position.
* **Resolution:** Add 1/2 cup water, check that the release valve is free of debris, ensure lid is rotated to the lock chime.

### D. Lid Will Not Open After Cooking
* **Root Cause:** Internal vacuum or residual pressure is keeping the mechanical lid lock engaged.
* **Resolution:** **NEVER force the lid.** Press the Safe Pressure Release Button to verify no steam remains. If float valve remains stuck up due to food residue, gently tap the lid handle with a spoon handle. Once the float drops, the lid will unlock smoothly.

---

## 2. Critical Safety Mechanisms

The Nutricook Smart Pot 2 incorporates 10+ hardware and software safety layers:
1. **Lid Lock Monitor:** Prevents pressure buildup if the lid is not fully rotated and engaged.
2. **Pressure Regulator Valve:** Regulates working internal pressure within safe thresholds (~70–80 kPa).
3. **Anti-Block Shield:** Prevents food particles from entering and obstructing the steam pipe.
4. **Auto-Seal Lid:** Automatically positions the steam exhaust to seal upon closing, eliminating human error.
5. **Overheat / Burn Sensor:** Cuts power to the heating element immediately if the pot bottom temperature rises excessively without sufficient liquid.
6. **Excess Pressure Release:** If secondary valves fail, the silicone gasket deforms to safely vent downward into the casing.
