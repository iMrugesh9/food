# Nutricook Smart Pot 2: Pressure Cooking Timetables

This reference details verified cooking times, liquid ratios, and release methods for grains, legumes, pulses, poultry, meats, and vegetables using the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 at **High Pressure**.

---

## 1. Grains, Rice & Pulses

| Grain / Lentil | Water Ratio (Grain : Water) | High Pressure Time | Release Protocol | Soak Needed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **White Basmati Rice** | 1 : 1.25 | 5–6 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Rinse until clear |
| **Jasmine / Sona Masoori**| 1 : 1.25 | 4–5 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Rinse |
| **Brown Rice** | 1 : 1.25 | 20–22 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Optional 30m soak |
| **Biryani (Chicken + Basmati)**| 1 : 1 (meat juices add fluid) | 6 min | 10-Min NR then QR | 30 min soak rice |
| **Toor Dal / Yellow Moong** | 1 : 3 | 7–8 min | 10-Min NR then QR | None |
| **Chana Dal / Split Bengal Gram** | 1 : 3 | 12–14 min | 10-Min NR then QR | 30 min soak |
| **Rajma (Kidney Beans) - Soaked** | 1 : 2.5 | 20–25 min | Full Natural Release | Soak 8+ hours |
| **Rajma - Unsoaked** | 1 : 3 | 45–50 min | Full Natural Release | None |
| **Kabuli Chana (Chickpeas) - Soaked**| 1 : 2.5 | 25–28 min | Full Natural Release | Soak 8+ hours |
| **Kabuli Chana - Unsoaked**| 1 : 3 | 50–55 min | Full Natural Release | None |
| **Whole Black Urad (Dal Makhani)**| 1 : 3.5 | 30–35 min | Full Natural Release | Soak 8+ hours |
| **Steel-Cut Oats** | 1 : 3 | 10 min | 10-Min NR then QR | None |
| **Quinoa** | 1 : 1.25 | 1–2 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Rinse |

## 2. Poultry & Meat

*Note: All meats should have at least 1 cup (250 ml) broth/water in the pot.*

| Meat Cut | Portion / Condition | High Pressure Time | Release Protocol | Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chicken Breast (Boneless)** | 2–3 pieces (~500g) | 8–10 min | 5-Min NR then QR | Shreds effortlessly |
| **Chicken Thighs (Bone-in)** | 4–6 thighs (~700g) | 12–14 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Juicier than breasts |
| **Whole Chicken** | 1.2 kg – 1.5 kg | 22–25 min | 15-Min NR then QR | Crisp skin in Faber Air Fryer after! |
| **Chicken Curry (Bone-in cuts)** | 600g | 8–10 min | 10-Min NR then QR | Sauté masala first |
| **Mutton / Goat Curry (Cubes)** | 500g – 750g | 22–25 min | Full Natural Release | Sauté meat 5 min first |
| **Beef Stew / Chuck Roast Cubes** | 750g | 30–35 min | Full Natural Release | Sear on Sauté More |
| **Pork Shoulder / Carnitas** | 1 kg (cubed) | 40–45 min | Full Natural Release | Fork-tender |
| **Hard-Boiled Eggs (5-5-5 Rule)** | 6 eggs on trivet + 1 cup water | 5 min | 5-Min NR -> QR -> 5-min Ice bath | Shells peel effortlessly |

## 3. Vegetables (Using Trivet & 1 Cup Water)

*Delicate vegetables require Low Pressure or minimal High Pressure with immediate Quick Release to avoid turning to mush.*

| Vegetable | Cut / Preparation | Pressure Level & Time | Release Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Potatoes (Whole, Large)** | Pricked with fork | High Pressure, 14–16 min | Quick Release |
| **Potatoes (Quartered / Cubed)** | 2-inch chunks | High Pressure, 4–5 min | Quick Release |
| **Sweet Potatoes (Whole)** | Medium size | High Pressure, 12–15 min | Quick Release |
| **Corn on the Cob** | Halved, on trivet | High Pressure, 2–3 min | Quick Release |
| **Beetroot (Whole)** | Medium size | High Pressure, 15–18 min | Quick Release |
| **Carrots / Cauliflower Florets** | 1-inch pieces | Low Pressure, 1–2 min | Quick Release |
| **Broccoli Florets** | Cut florets | Low Pressure, 0–1 min (or Steam mode) | Immediate Quick Release |
