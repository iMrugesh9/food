# Dual-Appliance Combo Workflows: Step-by-Step Guides

Detailed instructions for high-impact recipes leveraging both the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 and the Faber 6L Digital Air Fryer.

---

## 1. Restaurant-Style Tandoori Chicken

### Ingredients:
* 800g chicken drumsticks or bone-in thigh cuts (deeply scored)
* Marinade 1: 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (rest 15 min)
* Marinade 2: 1/2 cup thick yogurt, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tbsp mustard oil, garam masala, kasuri methi, cumin powder (marinate 2 hrs)
* 1 cup water (for pressure steaming)

### Procedure:
1. **Nutricook Smart Pot 2 (Par-cook & tenderize):**
   * Pour 1 cup water into the stainless pot. Insert trivet.
   * Arrange marinated chicken pieces on the trivet.
   * Lock Smart Lid. Select **Pressure Cook (High)** for **6 minutes**.
   * When complete, perform **Quick Release**. The chicken is fully cooked, sanitary, and juicy, with the yogurt marinade clinging to the meat.
2. **Faber 6L Air Fryer (Tandoor charring):**
   * Preheat Faber Air Fryer to **200°C** for 3 minutes.
   * Transfer chicken pieces onto the crisper plate in a single layer.
   * Brush pieces lightly with melted ghee or butter.
   * Air fry at **200°C for 6–8 minutes**, flipping at the 4-minute mark.
   * Observe through the viewing window until edges acquire classic charred tandoori spots.
3. **Finish:** Sprinkle with chaat masala and fresh coriander.

## 2. Crispy Pork / Lamb Carnitas

### Ingredients:
* 1 kg pork shoulder or lamb shoulder (cut into 2-inch chunks)
* 1 cup orange juice + 2 tbsp lime juice
* 1 onion (quartered), 4 cloves garlic (smashed), 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 2 tsp salt

### Procedure:
1. **Nutricook Smart Pot 2:**
   * Add seasoned meat cubes, juices, onion, and garlic to the pot.
   * Lock Smart Lid. Set to **Pressure Cook (High)** for **40 minutes**.
   * Allow **Full Natural Release** (approx. 20 minutes) so meat absorbs cooking juices.
   * Remove meat with tongs onto a cutting board. Shred coarsely with two forks.
2. **Reduce Broth (Nutricook Sauté):**
   * Switch Nutricook to **Sauté (More)**. Simmer remaining braising liquid for 8–10 minutes until reduced by half into a savory glaze.
3. **Faber 6L Air Fryer (Crisp & Crackle):**
   * Preheat Faber Air Fryer to **200°C**.
   * Spread shredded meat in the air fryer basket (do in two batches if necessary).
   * Drizzle 3–4 tbsp of reduced pot juices over the shredded meat.
   * Air fry at **200°C for 5–7 minutes** until the edges are crispy, caramelized, and fragrant.
