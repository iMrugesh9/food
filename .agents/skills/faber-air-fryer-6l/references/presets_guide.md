# Faber 6L Digital Air Fryer: Presets & Cooking Guide

This reference provides exact temperatures, cooking times, shake schedules, and preparation notes for a wide variety of ingredients cooked in the Faber 6L Air Fryer.

---

## 1. Poultry & Meats

| Item | Weight / Portion | Temperature (°C) | Time (Minutes) | Technique & Flipping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chicken Wings** | 500g – 750g | 190°C | 20–22 min | Shake at 10m & 16m; increase to 200°C for last 2 min for extra crunch |
| **Chicken Drumsticks** | 4–6 pieces | 180°C | 22–25 min | Flip at 12m; internal temp 75°C |
| **Chicken Breast** | 2 fillets (boneless) | 180°C | 15–18 min | Light oil rub; flip at 9m; rest 5 min before carving |
| **Tandoori Chicken Tikka** | 400g cubes | 190°C | 14–16 min | Skewer or space evenly; brush with melted ghee at 10m |
| **Lamb / Mutton Chops** | 4 chops (~400g) | 190°C | 14–18 min | Flip halfway; sear exterior |
| **Meatballs / Kebabs** | 10–12 pieces | 180°C | 12–14 min | Shake basket gently at 7m |
| **Bacon / Pancetta** | 6–8 strips | 180°C | 7–10 min | Drain grease from bottom pan halfway through if needed |

## 2. Seafood

| Item | Weight / Portion | Temperature (°C) | Time (Minutes) | Technique & Flipping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fish Fillets (Salmon/Cod)** | 2 fillets (~350g) | 175°C | 10–13 min | Skin-side down first; flip carefully at 7m |
| **Whole Small Fish (Pomfret)** | 1–2 fish (scored) | 185°C | 15–18 min | Brush with marinade; flip at 9m |
| **Prawns / Jumbo Shrimp** | 300g–400g | 175°C | 7–9 min | Toss with garlic butter; flip at 4m |
| **Fish Fingers (Frozen)** | 10–12 pieces | 200°C | 10–12 min | Shake basket at 6m; do not overcrowd |
| **Calamari Rings** | 250g | 190°C | 6–8 min | Breaded; spray with oil; shake at 4m |

## 3. Vegetarian, Paneer & Tofu

| Item | Weight / Portion | Temperature (°C) | Time (Minutes) | Technique & Flipping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paneer Tikka Cubes** | 300g (marinated) | 190°C | 10–12 min | Brush with ghee; turn pieces at 6m |
| **Crispy Tofu Cubes** | 300g (pressed, tossed in cornstarch)| 200°C | 14–16 min | Single layer; shake every 5 min |
| **French Fries (Fresh cut)** | 500g | 195°C | 18–22 min | Soak 30m, dry thoroughly, toss with 1 tbsp oil; shake 3 times |
| **French Fries (Frozen)** | 400g | 200°C | 14–16 min | No added oil needed; shake twice |
| **Potato Wedges** | 450g | 190°C | 18–20 min | Parboil 5 min or soak; toss with rosemary & oil; shake at 10m |
| **Cauliflower Florets (Gobi)** | 350g | 185°C | 12–15 min | Spiced oil toss; shake at 7m |
| **Broccoli Florets** | 300g | 175°C | 8–10 min | Add 1 tsp water under crisper plate to keep florets tender |
| **Roasted Mushrooms** | 250g | 180°C | 10–12 min | Toss in olive oil and herbs; shake at 6m |
| **Corn on the Cob** | 2 cobs (halved) | 190°C | 12–14 min | Brush with butter; rotate at 6m |

## 4. Snacks, Baking & Reheating

| Item | Temperature (°C) | Time (Minutes) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frozen Samosas / Spring Rolls** | 185°C | 12–15 min | Brush exterior with light oil for blistered crunch |
| **Frozen Nuggets / Smiles** | 200°C | 10–12 min | Shake basket at 5m |
| **Reheating Leftover Pizza** | 160°C | 3–5 min | Cheese melts perfectly, crust becomes crisper than original delivery |
| **Grilled Cheese / Toast** | 180°C | 5–7 min | Toothpick the bread to prevent airflow moving top slice; flip at 3m |
| **Muffins / Cupcakes** | 160°C | 15–18 min | Use silicone cups; test center with toothpick |
| **Brownies (6-inch tin)** | 150°C | 22–25 min | Place small cake tin directly on crisper plate |
