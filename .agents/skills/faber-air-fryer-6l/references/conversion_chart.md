# Faber 6L Air Fryer: Recipe Conversion Chart

Use these standardized mathematical rules to adapt conventional oven, stovetop deep-frying, and microwave recipes for the Faber 6.0L Digital Air Fryer.

---

## 1. Conventional Oven to Air Fryer Formula

Because the Faber 6L utilizes rapid 360° forced convection in a compact chamber:
1. **Temperature Rule:**
   $$\text{Air Fryer Temp (°C)} = \text{Oven Temp (°C)} - 15\text{°C to } 20\text{°C}$$
   *(Example: 200°C in an oven becomes 180°C or 185°C in the air fryer)*

2. **Cooking Time Rule:**
   $$\text{Air Fryer Time} = \text{Oven Time} \times 0.75 \text{ to } 0.80$$
   *(Example: A 40-minute oven roast chicken dish cooks in ~30–32 minutes)*

3. **Check Early:**
   Always start checking visual progress through the Faber viewing window at **70% of the calculated time**.

---

## 2. Deep-Frying to Air Fryer Conversion

| Deep-Fry Characteristic | Air Fryer Adaptation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wet Liquid Batter** (e.g. tempura, pakora, thin beer batter) | **Do not use directly.** Batter will drip through crisper plate holes. Instead: dredge in flour -> egg wash -> coarse breadcrumbs/cornflakes/suji (semolina). |
| **Coated Frozen Items** (nuggets, patties, frozen fries) | Place directly into preheated air fryer; no extra oil required as they already contain par-fried fats. |
| **Fresh Cut Veg / Meats** | Toss with **1 tablespoon (15 ml) oil per 500g** food. Even oil distribution is vital for heat conduction. |
| **Seasoning Timing** | Apply dry rubs and fine salts **before cooking** mixed with oil. Avoid light leafy herbs that can blow around and burn on the upper heating element. |

## 3. Quick Conversion Reference Table

| Conventional Oven Setting | Faber Air Fryer Equivalent | Typical Use Cases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **230°C (450°F)** | **200°C (Max)**, reduce time by 25% | Extra crispy roast potatoes, chicken wings, flash-seared steak |
| **200°C (400°F)** | **180°C – 185°C**, reduce time by 20% | Roasted vegetables, chicken breasts, sausages, pastries |
| **180°C (350°F)** | **160°C**, reduce time by 20% | Cakes, brownies, whole fish, stuffed capsicum |
| **150°C (300°F)** | **130°C – 140°C**, reduce time by 15% | Slow-rendered meats, beef jerky, low-temp fruit dehydration |
