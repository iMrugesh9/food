# Faber 6L Air Fryer: Troubleshooting & Maintenance

## 1. Common Operational Issues

### A. White Smoke Coming Out of Exhaust
* **Cause:** Cooking high-fat foods (bacon, sausage, fatty burger patties, fatty chicken skin). Excess oil drips into the bottom outer pan and burns at 180°C–200°C.
* **Solution:**
  1. Add 2–3 tablespoons (30–45 ml) of water or a slice of sandwich bread to the bottom of the outer pan underneath the crisper plate to absorb fat drips.
  2. Pause and drain excess grease between batches.
  3. Inspect heating element after cooling and wipe away oil deposits.

### B. Dark / Black Smoke
* **Cause:** Food or parchment paper made direct contact with the upper heating coil.
* **Solution:**
  1. Turn off and unplug the appliance immediately.
  2. Never use loose parchment paper without enough food weighting it down—convective airflow will lift it into the coil.
  3. Ensure food items do not exceed the max height limit of the basket (~8–9 cm).

### C. Uneven Browning or Soggy Food
* **Cause:** Overcrowding, lack of preheating, or skipping the mid-cycle shake/flip.
* **Solution:**
  1. Cook in batches rather than piling items high.
  2. Always preheat 3–5 minutes at target temperature.
  3. Shake every 5–7 minutes for fries and small items.

### D. Basket Difficult to Slide In or Lock
* **Cause:** Crisper tray improperly seated or misalignment of the sliding rail.
* **Solution:** Ensure the crisper plate rubber bumpers sit flat against the basket sides and press basket handle firmly until a distinct click is heard.

## 2. Safety & Care Principles

1. **Non-Stick Preservation:**
   * Never spray aerosol cooking sprays (e.g. Pam) directly onto the basket—propellants create an invisible sticky film that degrades non-stick surfaces over time.
   * Use an oil mister or pump bottle with pure oil (vegetable, mustard, olive, peanut, avocado).
   * Use silicone or bamboo utensils. Never use metal tongs or forks inside the basket.
2. **Thermal Protection:** Keep at least 15 cm (6 inches) of clearance on all sides and above the air fryer exhaust vents. Never operate directly under unvented wooden cabinets.
