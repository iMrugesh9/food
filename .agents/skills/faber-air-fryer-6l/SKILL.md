# Faber 6L Digital Air Fryer Operating Skill

## 1. Hardware Overview & Specifications

* **Model Series:** Faber FAF 6.0L Neo / VM / VW BK Series
* **Usable Capacity:** 6.0 Litres (Spacious single-basket format suitable for 4–6 servings or batch cooking)
* **Power Rating:** 1500 Watts (220V–240V AC, 50/60 Hz)
* **Temperature Range:** 80°C to 200°C (adjustable in 5°C increments)
* **Timer Range:** 1 minute to 60 minutes
* **Air Circulation:** 360° Rapid Air Technology / 200°C Swirl Convection for uniform crisping with up to 85% less oil
* **Basket System:** Detachable non-stick sliding frying pan with safety button release lock and crisper plate
* **Monitoring:** Viewing window with integrated internal light to check browning without interrupting airflow
* **Display & Control:** Digital LED touch control panel with auto-pause memory when the basket is pulled out

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### A. Preheating (Critical for Crispiness)
1. Always preheat the empty basket for **3–5 minutes at the target cooking temperature** unless reheating delicate foods.
2. Preheating ensures instantaneous crust formation on proteins and starch, sealing moisture inside while crisping the exterior.

### B. Basket Loading & Capacity Guidelines
1. **Never overcrowd:** Air fryers require high-velocity airflow. Leave at least 20–30% empty space for air circulation.
2. **Single Layer Only:** Spread fries, nuggets, patties, and chicken cuts in a single layer. For large batches, cook in consecutive cycles.
3. **Oil Application:** Lightly brush or mist cooking oil (avocado, ghee, olive, peanut) directly onto food. **Do not pour liquid oil into the bottom of the pan**. Avoid aerosol cooking sprays containing propellants (lecithin) that degrade the non-stick coating.

### C. The Mid-Cook Shake / Flip Routine
1. Pull the basket out at the **halfway mark** (or 2/3 mark). The heating element automatically pauses and timer freezes.
2. Shake vigorously for loose items (fries, roasted chickpeas, roasted vegetables) or flip using silicone/wooden tongs for proteins (chicken fillets, fish, paneer slices, kebabs).
3. Push the basket back until it clicks; the cooking cycle automatically resumes.

### D. Visual Monitoring
1. Tap the light button to illuminate the viewing window. Check caramelization and browning without releasing heat or pausing cooking.

## 4. Converting Recipes to Faber 6L Air Fryer

When converting conventional oven recipes:
* **Reduce Temperature:** Subtract **15°C to 20°C** from conventional oven recipe temperature (e.g., 200°C oven -> 180°C air fryer).
* **Reduce Time:** Reduce cook time by **20% to 25%**. Check food 5 minutes early.
* **Liquid Warning:** Do not use wet batters (tempura/thin besan) directly on the wire tray—they drip through. Use panko, breadcrumbs, coarse coating, or parchment paper with perforations.

## 5. Cleaning & Maintenance Runbook

1. **Unplug & Cool Down:** Always let the unit cool down completely (at least 20–30 min) before washing.
2. **Basket Care:**
   * Detach the basket using the safety button release guard.
   * Soak in warm water with mild dish detergent for 10 minutes if sticky residue is present.
   * Wash strictly with non-scratch sponge or microfiber cloth. **Never use steel wool or abrasive green pads**.
3. **Heating Coil Inspection:** Once cooled, invert or check the top heating element with a flashlight. Wipe off any grease splatters with a damp microfiber cloth to prevent smoke during high-heat cooking.
