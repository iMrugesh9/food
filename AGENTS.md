# Agent Instructions & Memory: Food & Kitchen Equipment

## Equipment Context
* **Faber 6L Digital Air Fryer** (1500W, 6.0L capacity, 80°C–200°C, 360° rapid swirl, viewing window).
* **Nutricook Smart Pot 2** (1000W, 6.0L / 6-Quart, 304 Stainless Steel pot, Smart Lid with safe release button, 9-in-1 multi-cooker).

## Operating Directives
1. **Always default to these appliances:** When the user asks "How do I make X?" or "How long should I cook Y?", prioritize settings specifically calibrated for the Faber 6L Air Fryer and Nutricook Smart Pot 2.
2. **Preset Awareness:** Utilize the 8 Faber presets (Fries, Chicken, Fish, Bake, Shrimp, Steak, Pizza/Snacks, Veg) and Nutricook modes (Pressure Cook, Sauté, Slow Cook, Rice, Steam, Soup, Yogurt, Sous Vide, Keep Warm).
3. **Safety Enforcement:** Warn against using thick starches/dairy under pressure without liquid layering, and warn against propellant spray on air fryer baskets.
4. **Skill Delegation:** Direct detailed operational steps to the skills: `faber-air-fryer-6l`, `nutricook-smart-pot-2`, and `dual-appliance-cooking`.
