## Overview & Philosophy

Modern cooking is transformed when an **electric pressure cooker** is paired with a **rapid-air digital air fryer**:
* **Nutricook Smart Pot 2** provides high-pressure steam infusion, rapid collagen breakdown, deep flavor absorption, hands-off grain/pulse simmering, and sous-vide precision.
* **Faber 6L Digital Air Fryer** provides 360° convective swirl heat, rapid surface moisture evaporation, Maillard browning, and ultra-crisp textures with up to 85% less oil.

Together, they eliminate 90% of stovetop splatter, active pan-tending, and excessive cooking oil while drastically speeding up meal prep.

### Core Operating Guidelines & Conversion Formulas

1. **Preheating:** Always preheat 3–5 minutes at the target temperature before adding food for instantaneous sear and crust formation.
2. **Spacing & Single Layer:** Do not pile food higher than one layer on the crisper plate. Convection requires uninhibited airflow around every piece.
3. **The Halfway Shake / Flip:** Shake loose items (fries, roasted chickpeas) or flip proteins (chicken, fish, paneer) halfway through cooking.
4. **Oven-to-Air Fryer Conversion:**
   * **Temperature:** Reduce conventional oven recipe temperature by **15°C to 20°C** ($T_{airfryer} = T_{oven} - 20\text{°C}$).
   * **Time:** Reduce conventional oven recipe time by **20% to 25%** ($t_{airfryer} = t_{oven} \times 0.75$).
5. **Oil Application:** Use an oil pump mister with pure oil (olive, avocado, mustard, peanut, ghee). **Never use aerosol sprays** (like Pam) with chemical propellants, as they leave an insoluble residue that ruins non-stick coatings.

---

### Maintenance & Cleaning
* **Cool Down First:** Allow basket to cool at least 20 minutes before contact with water.
* **Cleaning Materials:** Wash the basket and crisper tray with warm water, mild liquid dish soap, and a soft sponge. Never use metal scouring pads or abrasive detergents.
* **Coil Inspection:** Check the overhead heating coil periodically and wipe with a damp microfiber cloth once completely cold.

### 9-in-1 Multifunctional Modes

1. **Pressure Cook:** High Pressure (~70–80 kPa, 115°C–118°C) and Low Pressure (~40–50 kPa, 110°C).
2. **Sauté:** 3 temperature tiers (`Less` ~135°C, `Normal` ~160°C, `More` ~175°C) with the lid open for searing, sweating aromatics, tempering spices, and deglazing.
3. **Slow Cook:** Gentle simmer for 4 to 12 hours (`Less` ~85°C, `Normal` ~90°C, `More` ~95°C).
4. **Rice / Grains:** Smart sensor profile for white, brown, basmati, and jasmine rice.
5. **Steam:** Gentle steaming of vegetables, fish fillets, and dumplings using the stainless trivet.
6. **Soup / Broth:** High pressure extraction of bones, vegetables, and broths without turbulent boiling.
7. **Yogurt:** Automated pasteurization (`Boil` at 83°C) and temperature-controlled incubation (`Normal` at 40°C–43°C for 8–10 hours).
8. **Sous Vide:** Precision water bath control from 25°C to 90°C (adjustable in 1°C steps).
9. **Keep Warm:** Automatic or manual thermal holding at 63°C–78°C for up to 10–24 hours.

---

### Pressure Release Protocols (QR vs NR)

* **Quick Release (QR):** Immediately press and engage the Safe Pressure Release Button. Steam vents in ~2–3 minutes until the float valve drops.
  * *Best for:* Quick-cooking vegetables, corn on the cob, seafood, hard-boiled eggs.
* **Natural Release (NR):** Do not touch the cooker after the timer ends. Let the pot depressurize naturally as it cools (15–30 minutes) until the float valve drops.
  * *Best for:* Large cuts of meat, bone broths, rajma, chana, high-starch soups.
* **10-Minute Natural Release:** Allow 10 minutes of natural release, then press the Safe Release Button to discharge remaining steam.
  * *Best for:* Chicken breasts, poultry curries, rice, biryani, pasta.

### Safety Safeguards & Burn Prevention

* **The Minimum Liquid Rule:** Always add at least **1 cup (250 ml)** of thin water or broth. Steam cannot generate without adequate liquid.
* **The Deglazing Mandate:** After sautéing onions, garlic, or searing meats, always splash 1/4 cup broth and scrape the bottom of the stainless pot clean with a wooden spatula. **Any fond stuck to the bottom will trigger the `Burn` error**.
* **Layering Thick Sauces:** Never mix tomato paste, purees, or cornstarch directly onto the bottom. Pour broth first, add meat/vegetables, and pour thick tomato purees **on top without stirring**.

## Dual-Appliance Synergy: The Hybrid Pipeline

### Two-Stage Cooking (Tenderize -> Crisp)
The greatest advantage of owning both appliances is pairing them in a continuous two-stage workflow:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐        ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│     STAGE 1: NUTRICOOK POT 2    │        │    STAGE 2: FABER AIR FRYER     │
│   (Hydration, Pressure & Tenderness)   │───────>│     (Maillard Reaction & Crunch)   │
│ • Breaks down collagen in 20 min │ Transfer│ • 190°C–200°C Rapid Swirl Air   │
│ • Leaves meat juicy & tender    │ Solids │ • Glazes, crisps skin, browns   │
│ • Generates rich bone broth     │        │ • Renders exterior fat          │
└─────────────────────────────────┘        └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Example: Crispy Melting Roast Potatoes
1. **Pressure Cook (Nutricook):** Quartered potatoes on trivet with 1 cup water, **High Pressure for 4 min**, Quick Release.
2. **Air Fry (Faber):** Toss par-cooked potatoes with olive oil, salt, garlic powder; air fry at **195°C for 12–15 min** shaking twice. Result: Fluffy interior, glass-like crackling exterior.

---

### Parallel Meal Execution
* **Cook Curry + Starters:** Simmer Dal Makhani or Chicken Curry in the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 while air-frying Paneer Tikka or Chicken Wings in the Faber 6L Air Fryer.
* **Main Grain + Roasted Veggies:** Cook Basmati Rice or Quinoa in the Nutricook while roasting spiced vegetables and crunchy chickpeas in the Faber.
